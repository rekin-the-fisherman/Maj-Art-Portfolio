MY COMMISSION PROCESS

HOW TO COMMISSION

Follow these instructions to successfully secure a commission slot!

Section 1

Terms and Conditions

♡
Payment & Refunds

Full payment is required upfront. Refunds are only available before work begins.

Revisions & Approval

No major changes to the original request. Corrections are allowed if the artwork does not follow your references. No changes after final approval.

Usage Rights & Credit

Personal use only. Commercial use costs an extra 90% of the base price. Always credit the artist when reposting.

Portfolio & Privacy

The artist may post completed artwork unless privacy is requested.

Content & Right to Decline

No NSFW or heavy gore. The artist may decline unsuitable requests.

Section 2

How To Commission

♡
Submit the Form

Fill out our Google Form with your commission request.

Confirmation & Queue

We will contact you through your preferred platform for the confirmation of your request and its scheduled start date.

Payment

We will inform you of the payment details after your request has been confirmed. Transactions must be settled on or before the scheduled date for your slot. Unpaid slots may be skipped.

Artwork & Approval

Updates and corrections will be handled through your preferred contact platform.

Final Delivery

Completed artwork will be sent through Gmail or Google Drive.
